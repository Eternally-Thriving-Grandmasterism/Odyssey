# Batch 5 — Books 13–16: Return to Ithaca & First Recognitions

**Visual Script + Grok Imagine Prompts**

---

## BOOK 13 — Arrival on Ithaca

### Scene 13.1 — The Phaeacian Ship & Landing
**Homeric core**: The Phaeacians bring Odysseus home while he sleeps. They leave him and his gifts on the shore of Ithaca and depart. Poseidon turns their ship to stone as punishment.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Moonlit or early morning shore of Ithaca. Odysseus lying asleep among the rich gifts of the Phaeacians. The Phaeacian ship already pulling away into the sea. In the distance the ship beginning to turn to stone under Poseidon’s anger. Quiet homecoming at last, divine justice on the helpers. Homeric irony and beauty.
```

### Scene 13.2 — Athena Reveals Herself & the Disguise
**Homeric core**: Odysseus wakes and does not recognize his own land. Athena appears, first in disguise, then reveals herself. She transforms him into an old beggar.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Rocky shore of Ithaca. Odysseus standing bewildered. Athena appearing first as a young shepherd, then revealing her true form with aegis and authority. She touches him and his appearance changes: skin wrinkles, hair thins, clothing becomes tattered beggar’s rags, staff in hand. Moment of divine strategy and the beginning of the final test. Pure Homeric transformation.
```

## BOOK 14 — The Hut of Eumaeus

### Scene 14.1 — Arrival at the Swineherd’s Hut
**Homeric core**: Odysseus, as beggar, is received with perfect xenia by the loyal swineherd Eumaeus.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Simple hut of Eumaeus the swineherd on the slopes of Ithaca. Eumaeus, older loyal servant, welcoming the disguised Odysseus with genuine hospitality: seat by the fire, food, cloak. Pigs in the yard, dogs (except the one that will later recognize), rough wooden interior. Atmosphere of pure, humble xenia contrasting with the suitors’ violation. Homeric warmth and fidelity.
```

### Scene 14.2 — Eumaeus Speaks of His Master
**Homeric core**: Eumaeus tells the “beggar” of his love and longing for Odysseus, and of the suitors’ outrages.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Interior of Eumaeus’ hut at night. Firelight. Disguised Odysseus listening intently as Eumaeus speaks with emotion of his absent master. Loyalty and grief visible. Quiet, powerful Homeric scene of unrecognized recognition.
```

## BOOK 15 — Telemachus Returns

### Scene 15.1 — Athena Warns Telemachus
**Homeric core**: Athena comes to Telemachus in Sparta and urges him to return home, warning of the ambush.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Chamber in Menelaus’ palace at night. Athena appearing to sleeping or waking Telemachus, urgent and protective. Sense of impending danger and the goddess’ care. Homeric divine intervention.
```

### Scene 15.2 — Telemachus Sails Home
**Homeric core**: Telemachus leaves Sparta, avoids the ambush, and lands on Ithaca.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Ship of Telemachus approaching the coast of Ithaca at night or dawn. Young prince at the tiller or watching the shore. Sense of homecoming for the son, parallel to the father’s. Bronze Age vessel, Mediterranean night.
```

## BOOK 16 — Father and Son

### Scene 16.1 — Telemachus at the Hut
**Homeric core**: Telemachus arrives at Eumaeus’ hut. The dogs do not bark at him. Odysseus (still disguised) watches his son.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Exterior of Eumaeus’ hut. Young Telemachus approaching. The dogs of the swineherd fawning on him instead of barking. Disguised Odysseus sitting nearby, eyes fixed on his son with hidden emotion. Moment of near-recognition and paternal love restrained. Homeric restraint and depth.
```

### Scene 16.2 — The Recognition
**Homeric core**: After Eumaeus is sent to the city, Athena restores Odysseus’ true appearance. Father and son embrace and weep.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Interior of the swineherd’s hut. Athena present. Odysseus transformed back to his true form: mature, bearded, powerful. Telemachus staring in shock, then father and son embracing tightly, both weeping. One of the most emotional scenes in the epic. Pure Homeric pathos, no modern sentimentality, Bronze Age clothing and setting.
```

### Scene 16.3 — Planning the Vengeance
**Homeric core**: Father and son begin to plan the destruction of the suitors.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Close conversation in the hut. Odysseus and Telemachus, now united, speaking low and intently. Weapons nearby. Atmosphere of resolve and the coming tisis (vengeance). Homeric strategy and justice beginning.
```
