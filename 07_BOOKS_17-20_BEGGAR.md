# Batch 6 — Books 17–20: The Beggar in His Own Hall

**Visual Script + Grok Imagine Prompts**

---

## BOOK 17 — Entry into the Palace

### Scene 17.1 — The Dog Argos
**Homeric core**: As Odysseus approaches his palace in beggar form, his old dog Argos, neglected and lying on a dung heap, recognizes him, wags his tail, and dies.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Outside the gates of Odysseus’ palace in Ithaca. Disguised Odysseus approaching. Old, flea-ridden dog Argos lying on a pile of dung and refuse, suddenly lifting his head, recognizing his master after twenty years, weakly wagging his tail. Odysseus turning his face away to hide a tear. One of the most moving moments in all literature. Pure Homeric pathos, Bronze Age setting, no sentimentality beyond the text.
```

### Scene 17.2 — Entry and the First Insults
**Homeric core**: Odysseus enters the hall as a beggar. The suitors mock him. Antinous strikes him with a footstool.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Interior of Odysseus’ great hall, filled with suitors feasting. Disguised Odysseus moving among them as a beggar, asking for scraps. Antinous, arrogant, hurling a footstool that strikes Odysseus. Other suitors laughing. Atmosphere of violated xenia at its worst. Homeric tension building toward justice.
```

## BOOK 18 — Further Tests

### Scene 18.1 — The Fight with Irus
**Homeric core**: The professional beggar Irus challenges Odysseus. Odysseus, still in disguise, easily defeats him.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Hall of Odysseus. Two beggars facing each other: the young, insolent Irus and the older, disguised Odysseus. Odysseus revealing sudden strength and striking Irus down. Suitors watching and laughing. Display of hidden power. Homeric physicality.
```

### Scene 18.2 — Penelope Appears Before the Suitors
**Homeric core**: Athena enhances Penelope’s beauty. She comes down to the hall, rebukes the suitors, and draws gifts from them.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Staircase or threshold of the women’s quarters opening onto the hall. Penelope, mature and regal, descending or standing before the suitors. Athena’s enhancement visible as a sudden radiance of beauty and dignity. Suitors stunned into silence or desire. Odysseus watching from among the beggars. Homeric moment of cunning and presence.
```

---

## BOOK 19 — Penelope and the Stranger

### Scene 19.1 — The Conversation by the Fire
**Homeric core**: Penelope questions the “beggar.” Odysseus tells a false Cretan tale but describes himself accurately enough that Penelope weeps. He predicts Odysseus’ return.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Quiet corner of the hall or side chamber at night. Penelope seated, the disguised Odysseus opposite her. Firelight. She weeping as he speaks of her husband. Deep emotional intimacy between husband and wife who do not yet openly recognize each other. Homeric restraint and power.
```

### Scene 19.2 — Eurycleia and the Scar
**Homeric core**: The old nurse Eurycleia washes the stranger’s feet and recognizes the scar from the boar hunt on Parnassus. Odysseus silences her.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Close interior scene. Old nurse Eurycleia washing the feet of the disguised Odysseus. Her hands find the scar on his leg; her eyes widen in recognition; she starts to cry out. Odysseus clamping a hand over her mouth, eyes intense. Moment of secret recognition. Pure Homeric detail and tension.
```

---

## BOOK 20 — The Final Night Before the Contest

### Scene 20.1 — Odysseus’ Sleepless Night
**Homeric core**: Odysseus lies awake in the hall, listening to the faithless maids go to the suitors, planning the slaughter, and receiving a sign from Zeus.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Darkened hall of Odysseus at night. Disguised Odysseus lying on a rough bed or hide near the entrance, eyes open, listening. Faithless serving women slipping out to the suitors. Atmosphere of impending judgment and divine support. Homeric interior tension.
```

### Scene 20.2 — The Portents
**Homeric core**: Thunder from a clear sky; a servant woman grinding grain prays that this be the last day of the suitors.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Early morning in the palace. Clear sky suddenly giving a clap of thunder. An old woman at the hand-mill looking up and praying that the suitors’ feast be their last. Odysseus hearing and taking heart. Divine sign confirming the coming tisis.
```
