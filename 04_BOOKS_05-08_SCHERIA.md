# Batch 3 — Books 5–8: From Ogygia to Scheria

**Visual Script + Grok Imagine Prompts**  
True to Homer. Bronze Age spirit. Divine order and xenia central.

---

## BOOK 5 — Calypso & the Raft

### Scene 5.1 — Hermes Arrives on Ogygia
**Homeric core**: Hermes, messenger of Zeus, comes to the island of Calypso to order the release of Odysseus.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Remote wooded island of Ogygia, late Bronze Age mythic spirit. Hermes in traveler’s guise with winged sandals and caduceus of the period, walking through lush forest toward Calypso’s cave. Soft dappled light, ancient trees, sense of isolation and divine mission. Epic Homeric atmosphere, no modern elements.
```

### Scene 5.2 — Calypso’s Cave & the Release
**Homeric core**: Calypso, the beautiful nymph, reluctantly agrees to let Odysseus go. She finds him weeping on the shore, gazing toward Ithaca.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Shore of Ogygia at evening. Odysseus, mature bearded sea-worn king, sitting alone on rocks, looking out to the empty sea with deep longing (nostos). Behind him the cave of Calypso, the nymph standing with mixed sorrow and obedience. Lush vegetation, golden late light, Mediterranean sea. Solemn, faithful to Homer, no anachronism, pure epic emotion.
```

### Scene 5.3 — Building the Raft
**Homeric core**: Calypso gives Odysseus tools; he fells trees and builds a large raft over four days.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Forest edge on Ogygia. Odysseus, strong and skilled, working with bronze tools of the period, shaping large logs into a raft. Calypso watching from a distance. Sunlight through trees, wood chips, effort and determination. Bronze Age craftsmanship, Homeric detail, no modern tools.
```

### Scene 5.4 — Poseidon’s Storm
**Homeric core**: After seventeen days at sea, Poseidon sees Odysseus and raises a terrible storm. Odysseus is nearly drowned; Ino (Leucothea) helps him; Athena calms the winds at last.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Open sea under dark storm clouds, late Bronze Age mythic realism. Odysseus clinging to the wrecked raft as huge waves crash. Poseidon, dark-bearded and terrible, rising from the depths with trident, wrathful. Dramatic lightning, salt spray, struggle for life. Epic scale, divine anger visible, no modern ships or effects. Homeric terror and endurance.
```

### Scene 5.5 — Landing on Scheria
**Homeric core**: Odysseus swims to the shore of the Phaeacians, exhausted, and hides under olive bushes.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Rocky shoreline of Scheria at night or early dawn. Odysseus, naked and exhausted, crawling ashore and collapsing beneath wild olive trees. Sea still restless behind him. Atmosphere of survival and the end of one ordeal. Pure Homeric landing, Bronze Age landscape.
```

## BOOK 6 — Nausicaa

### Scene 6.1 — Athena Visits Nausicaa in a Dream
**Homeric core**: Athena appears to the princess Nausicaa in a dream, urging her to wash clothes by the river.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Interior of the palace of Alcinous, night. Young Nausicaa sleeping. Athena appearing in dream form, luminous and gentle, speaking. Soft divine light, Bronze Age bedroom with simple furnishings and textiles. Epic, no anachronism.
```

### Scene 6.2 — Nausicaa at the River
**Homeric core**: Nausicaa and her maidens wash clothes, play ball, and Odysseus emerges from the bushes, covering himself with a branch.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
River mouth near the sea on Scheria, bright morning. Nausicaa, young princess of beauty and dignity, with her maidens washing linen by the water and playing. Odysseus, bearded and weathered, emerging cautiously from the thicket, holding a leafy branch for modesty. Atmosphere of sudden encounter, wonder, and proper restraint. Homeric xenia beginning, pure Bronze Age Mediterranean light.
```

### Scene 6.3 — Nausicaa Gives Clothing and Guidance
**Homeric core**: She gives him oil and clothing, and advises him how to approach her parents without causing scandal.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
By the river on Scheria. Nausicaa standing with quiet authority, handing clean linen garments to Odysseus who kneels or stands respectfully. Maidens nearby. Soft morning light, sense of generous yet careful hospitality. Epic Homeric moment of first xenia on the island.
```

## BOOK 7 — The Palace of Alcinous

### Scene 7.1 — Odysseus Enters the City in Mist
**Homeric core**: Athena covers Odysseus in a mist so he can reach the palace unseen.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Streets of the Phaeacian city, Bronze Age style. Odysseus walking, partially veiled in a soft divine mist. People going about daily life. Sense of protected arrival. Homeric atmosphere.
```

### Scene 7.2 — The Marvelous Palace & Gardens
**Homeric core**: Description of the splendid palace of Alcinous with bronze walls, gold and silver dogs, and the ever-bearing orchard.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Exterior and threshold of the palace of Alcinous on Scheria. Mycenaean-inspired grandeur: bronze-sheathed doors or walls in Homeric spirit, golden and silver guardian dogs at the entrance, rich frescoes, and beyond them the famous orchard with fruit trees of every kind in perpetual season. Odysseus approaching in wonder. Luminous, abundant, faithful to the text’s description of Phaeacian prosperity and xenia.
```

### Scene 7.3 — Supplication at the Hearth
**Homeric core**: Odysseus enters, throws his arms around the knees of Queen Arete, and sits in the ashes of the hearth as a suppliant.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Interior megaron of Alcinous. Odysseus, still travel-worn, kneeling at the central hearth, arms around the knees of Queen Arete who sits in dignity. King Alcinous and the Phaeacian nobles present. Firelight, bronze vessels, high status textiles, atmosphere of sacred supplication and the beginning of high xenia. Pure Homeric ritual moment.
```

## BOOK 8 — Games, Song, and Tears

### Scene 8.1 — The Games of the Phaeacians
**Homeric core**: Athletic contests: running, wrestling, jumping, discus, boxing. Odysseus is challenged and throws the discus farthest.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Open ground near the city of the Phaeacians. Young men competing in athletic games of the period. Odysseus, mature and powerful, stepping forward to throw a heavy stone discus with great force. Spectators, including Alcinous. Mediterranean light, Bronze Age clothing, epic athletic spirit without later classical stadium architecture.
```

### Scene 8.2 — Demodocus Sings of Troy
**Homeric core**: The blind bard Demodocus sings of the quarrel of Odysseus and Achilles, then of the Trojan Horse. Odysseus weeps, hiding his face.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Banquet hall of Alcinous at night. Blind bard Demodocus seated, singing to the lyre. Odysseus, guest of honor, turning his face away and weeping as the song of Troy and his own deeds is performed. Soft firelight and oil lamps, Phaeacian nobles listening. Deep emotion of nostos and memory. Homeric epic pathos, no modern instruments or clothing.
```

### Scene 8.3 — Odysseus Reveals His Name
**Homeric core**: Pressed by Alcinous, Odysseus begins to tell his true identity and story.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Close moment in the hall. Odysseus, face still marked by tears, looking up and beginning to speak. Alcinous and Arete listening with full attention. Atmosphere of recognition and the opening of the long tale. Solemn, luminous, pure Homeric revelation.
```
