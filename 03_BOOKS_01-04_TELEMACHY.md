## BOOK 1 — The Council of the Gods & Athena in Ithaca

### Scene 1.1 — Olympus: The Gods Deliberate
**Homeric core**: Zeus speaks of Aegisthus and the folly of men. Athena intercedes for Odysseus, held by Calypso.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Epic Homeric scene on Mount Olympus, late Bronze Age Aegean mythic spirit. Zeus, majestic bearded king of the gods, seated on a simple high throne of stone and wood, holding a scepter, speaking with solemn authority. Athena stands before him, grey-eyed, wearing a simple bronze-age style aegis over a linen tunic, no classical helmet with crest of later periods, calm and resolute. Other gods present in hierarchical scale, ethereal yet solid presence. Soft divine radiance, Mediterranean mountain light, fresco-like clarity, no modern elements, pure epic atmosphere of divine order. Solemn, luminous, no anachronism.
```

**Homeric core**: The suitors of Penelope feast daily in Odysseus’ hall, consuming his livestock and wine, while Telemachus sits apart in grief.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Interior of a Mycenaean-style megaron hall in Ithaca, late Bronze Age spirit. Young Telemachus, early twenties, seated near a central hearth, looking downward with quiet anger and sorrow. Around him arrogant young suitors recline on couches and seats, eating roasted meat, drinking wine from cups, laughing loudly, servants attending. Heavy wooden columns, frescoed walls with geometric and animal motifs, oil lamps and firelight, bronze vessels, linen and wool clothing of the period, no classical architecture, no modern faces or clothing. Atmosphere of violated xenia and tension.
```

### Scene 1.3 — Athena as Mentes Visits Telemachus
**Homeric core**: Athena arrives disguised as Mentes, lord of the Taphians. She encourages Telemachus and plants the idea of seeking news of his father.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Courtyard or threshold of Odysseus’ palace in Ithaca, Bronze Age Aegean. Athena in mortal guise as Mentes: mature traveler with spear and simple cloak, standing before young Telemachus. Telemachus rises to offer hospitality (xenia). Soft afternoon light, stone walls, olive trees in background, solemn conversation. Epic Homeric atmosphere, no anachronisms, pure mythic realism.
```

## BOOK 2 — The Assembly & Departure

### Scene 2.1 — The Assembly of the Ithacans
**Homeric core**: Telemachus calls the assembly. He speaks against the suitors. Antinous and Eurymachus reply with insolence. Penelope’s weaving of the shroud is described.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Open-air assembly place in Ithaca, late Bronze Age. Telemachus standing before seated elders and people, speaking with rising courage. The suitors clustered together, Antinous prominent and sneering. Simple stone seats or ground, Mediterranean landscape, olive and cypress, no classical agora columns. Atmosphere of tension between rightful order and insolence. Homeric epic clarity.
```

### Scene 2.2 — Penelope’s Loom (the ruse)
**Homeric core**: Penelope weaves a great shroud for Laertes by day and unravels it by night for three years.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Interior chamber of Penelope in the palace of Ithaca. Mature, dignified Penelope seated at a large vertical loom of Bronze Age type, weaving a white shroud with geometric patterns. Soft indoor light from high windows, wooden beams, textiles, quiet concentration and cunning. No modern furniture, pure Homeric domestic world. Atmosphere of patient resistance and fidelity.
```

### Scene 2.3 — Athena as Mentor Prepares the Ship
**Homeric core**: Athena, now as Mentor, helps Telemachus gather a crew and outfit a ship.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Harbor of Ithaca at dusk or dawn, Bronze Age ship being prepared. Open-hulled vessel with high stem and stern, oars, single mast. Young Telemachus and loyal companions loading provisions. Athena in the guise of Mentor, older man, giving quiet direction. Salt air, rocky coastline, Mediterranean light. Epic, no modern ships or elements.
```


### Scene 3.2 — Nestor’s Hall & Storytelling
**Homeric core**: Nestor receives Telemachus with full xenia and tells of the returns from Troy and the murder of Agamemnon.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:

```
Megaron of Nestor at Pylos, Mycenaean palace spirit. Aged Nestor seated, speaking to Telemachus who listens intently. Central hearth fire, frescoed walls, bronze vessels, servants, atmosphere of hospitality and oral tradition. Solemn firelight, epic storytelling mood.
```

**Homeric core**: Telemachus and Peisistratus arrive at the splendid palace of Menelaus during a double wedding feast. Helen recognizes the likeness to Odysseus.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Grand megaron of Menelaus in Sparta, rich but still Bronze Age in spirit. Menelaus and Helen receiving the young guests. Helen of renowned beauty, mature, with golden ornaments of the period. Tables of feast, gold and silver vessels (Homeric description), atmosphere of high xenia and lingering Trojan memory. Luminous interior, no classical columns of later eras.
```

### Scene 4.2 — Menelaus Tells of Proteus & Odysseus on Calypso’s Isle
**Homeric core**: Menelaus recounts his own return and the news from the Old Man of the Sea that Odysseus is held by Calypso.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Intimate moment in Menelaus’ hall. Menelaus speaking seriously to Telemachus, Helen nearby. Soft evening light, sense of long years and hard-won knowledge. Epic Homeric atmosphere of revelation.
```

**Homeric core**: Back in Ithaca the suitors learn of Telemachus’ voyage and plan to intercept him at sea.

**Grok Imagine Prompt**:
```
Darkened courtyard or side chamber of Odysseus’ palace at night. Antinous and other leading suitors plotting in low voices, faces lit by oil lamp. Atmosphere of conspiracy and violated xenia. Bronze Age interior, no modern elements.
```
