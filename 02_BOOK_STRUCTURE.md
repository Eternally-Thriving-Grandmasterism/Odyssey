# The Odyssey — Book Structure & Visual Priority

Faithful outline for visual scripting. Each book will receive dedicated Grok Imagine prompts in later batches.

## Part I — The Telemachy (Books 1–4)
**Focus**: Ithaca under siege by suitors; Telemachus’ coming of age; search for news of his father.

- **Book 1**: Council of the gods. Athena visits Telemachus in Ithaca disguised as Mentes. The suitors feast outrageously.
- **Book 2**: Assembly of the Ithacans. Telemachus speaks. Penelope’s weaving ruse revealed. Athena (as Mentor) helps prepare the ship.
- **Book 3**: Telemachus at Pylos with Nestor. Sacrifice and storytelling. Journey to Sparta begins.
- **Book 4**: Telemachus at Sparta with Menelaus and Helen. News of Odysseus held by Calypso. Suitors plot ambush.

## Part II — From Ogygia to Scheria (Books 5–8)
**Focus**: Odysseus released; shipwreck; arrival among the Phaeacians; xenia at its highest.

- **Book 5**: Hermes to Calypso. Odysseus builds the raft. Poseidon’s storm. Landing on Scheria.
- **Book 6**: Nausicaa at the river. Odysseus appears. She guides him toward the city.
- **Book 7**: Odysseus in the palace of Alcinous and Arete. Supplication at the hearth.
- **Book 8**: Games, feast, Demodocus sings of Troy. Odysseus weeps and is recognized by emotion.

## Part III — The Apologoi (Books 9–12)
**Focus**: Odysseus tells his own story to the Phaeacians.

- **Book 9**: Cicones, Lotus-Eaters, Cave of Polyphemus (blinding).
- **Book 10**: Aeolus, Laestrygonians, Circe.
- **Book 11**: The Nekyia — journey to the land of the dead; Teiresias; shades of the heroes and kin.
- **Book 12**: Sirens, Scylla & Charybdis, Cattle of Helios, final shipwreck, arrival at Ogygia.

## Part IV — Return & Recognition (Books 13–16)
- **Book 13**: Phaeacian ship to Ithaca. Odysseus wakes on his own soil. Athena reveals herself and plans the disguise.
- **Book 14**: At the hut of Eumaeus the swineherd. Xenia of the loyal servant.
- **Book 15**: Telemachus returns from Sparta. Reunion of father and son at the hut (Book 16).

## Part V — The Beggar in His Own Hall (Books 17–20)
- Odysseus enters the palace as beggar. Tests of loyalty. Encounters with Antinous, Eurymachus, Melanthius, the maids, Penelope, and the dog Argos.

## Part VI — The Bow & the Slaughter (Books 21–24)
- **Book 21**: The contest of the bow.
- **Book 22**: The killing of the suitors. Purification.
- **Book 23**: Recognition by Penelope (the olive-tree bed).
- **Book 24**: Laertes, the final confrontation with the kin of the suitors, Athena’s peace.
