# Fidelity Protocol — The Odyssey Visual Script

**PATSAGi Councils Standing Order**  
Every image generated from this repository must obey the following without exception.

## 1. Textual Fidelity
- Follow the sequence and spirit of Homer’s 24 books.
- Key dialogue moments, epithets, and actions must remain recognizable.
- Do not invent modern psychological subtext that contradicts the text.

## 2. Cultural & Mythic Fidelity
- **Nostos**: The longing for home is the emotional and narrative engine.
- **Xenia**: Hospitality is a sacred law of Zeus. Violation brings divine wrath. Proper xenia is shown with warmth, gifts, and ritual.
- **Divine Order**: The gods are present and active. Athena is Odysseus’ steadfast ally. Poseidon is his enemy because of Polyphemus. Zeus holds the balance.

## 3. Visual Period Spirit (Bronze Age Memory)
Although the poems were written down centuries later, the *world* they describe must feel like the late Bronze Age Aegean as remembered in epic tradition:
- Architecture: Mycenaean-style megarons, heavy stone, painted frescoes, high thresholds.
- Clothing: Simple linen or wool tunics (chitons), pinned, belted; longer robes for women of status; no classical draped peploi of the 5th century.
- Armor: When armor appears, prefer bronze plate elements and especially the boar’s-tusk helmet (explicitly described for Odysseus in the *Iliad* and appropriate here).
- Ships: Open-hulled, high stem and stern, single square sail or oared, following the spirit of the Thera flotilla frescoes and Homeric descriptions — not classical triremes.
- Weapons: Bronze swords, spears, bows of the period.

## 4. Absolute Prohibitions
- No modern clothing, watches, firearms, vehicles, or architecture.
- No celebrity likenesses or “cast as” instructions.
- No anachronistic classical Greek temples, columns of later orders, or Roman elements.
- No steampunk, fantasy-metal, or high-fantasy embellishment that breaks Homeric tone.
- No soft modern lighting or cinematic color grading that feels 21st-century.

## 5. Character Appearance Rules
- **Odysseus**: Mature man (late 30s–40s by the time of return), bearded, weathered skin from sea and sun, intelligent sharp eyes, strong build but not bodybuilder, often in simple traveler’s or beggar’s clothing when disguised.
- **Penelope**: Dignified, mature, beautiful but marked by years of waiting and cunning. Not a young ingenue.
- **Telemachus**: Young man coming into his strength (early 20s).
- **Gods**: Larger than life, radiant or terrible according to nature, but still recognizably anthropomorphic within the epic tradition. Athena often appears in mortal guise (Mentes, Mentor, etc.).

## 6. Prompt Engineering Rule
Every Grok Imagine prompt in this repository already embeds the above constraints. Do not dilute them.
