# Visual Style Guide — Bronze Age Spirit for Grok Imagine

## Overall Aesthetic
Epic, solemn, luminous, and earthy.  
Think of the surviving frescoes of Thera, Pylos, and Mycenae filtered through Homeric imagination: rich earth pigments, strong outlines, hierarchical scale for gods and heroes, deep Mediterranean light, salt air, stone, bronze, linen, and blood.

### Lighting
- Natural Mediterranean sunlight or firelight inside halls.
- Divine presence often announced by a sudden clarity or golden radiance that does not look like modern lens flare.
- Night scenes: stars, fire, oil lamps.

### Color Palette
- Earth reds, ochres, deep blues of the sea, white of linen and marble/stone, bronze greens and dark oxidized metal, black of hair and shadows, occasional purple of high status dye.

### Materials to Emphasize
- Bronze (dark, heavy, sometimes dented and living with use — not shiny gold fantasy metal).
- Linen and wool textiles with visible weave.
- Wood of ships and furniture.
- Rough or dressed stone of palaces and walls.
- Leather, ivory (boar’s tusks), gold ornaments sparingly for kings and gods.

### Architecture
- Mycenaean-inspired: megaron halls with central hearth, columns of wood or stone, frescoed walls, high thresholds, cyclopean masonry where fortresses appear.
- No later classical peristyle temples or Ionic/Corinthian orders.

### Ships
- Long, open vessels with high curved stem and stern posts, often decorated with animal heads in spirit of the period.
- Rowers on benches, single mast with square sail when under sail.
- No multi-level war galleys of later centuries.

### Clothing & Adornment
**Men**: Short or knee-length linen tunics, sometimes with patterned borders; belts; cloaks (chlaina); simple leather sandals or barefoot. Kings may wear more richly dyed and embroidered garments.
**Women**: Longer robes, often pinned at shoulders, with belts; hair elaborately arranged or covered; jewelry of gold and glass paste for high status.
**Warriors**: When armored — bronze elements, boar’s-tusk helmets for elite (especially Odysseus when appropriate), large body shields or figure-of-eight shields in early spirit, spears, swords.

### Gods
- Athena: Often in mortal guise, but when revealed — aegis, owl associations, grey eyes, commanding presence.
- Poseidon: Dark-bearded, powerful, associated with the sea and horses, trident.
- Zeus: Majestic, bearded, holding the balance of fate.
- Appearances of gods should feel awe-inspiring yet continuous with the human world, never cartoonish.

### Atmosphere Keywords for Prompts
“Homeric epic atmosphere”, “late Bronze Age Aegean spirit”, “Mycenaean palace world memory”, “salt-stained and sun-weathered”, “sacred xenia”, “divine presence without modern spectacle”, “solemn and luminous”, “no anachronism”.
